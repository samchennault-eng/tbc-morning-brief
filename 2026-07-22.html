Björk: The Total Artist
Alex Ross · The New Yorker · 2004

Ross treats Björk not as a pop star dabbling in the avant-garde but as a composer whose ear moves freely between medieval polyphony, Inuit throat singing, and glitchy electronics. The argument: genre is a marketing convenience, not a limit on what a serious musician can absorb. Two decades on, his case for the artist who refuses to pick a lane reads less like a profile than a manifesto.

AI Transparency Deadline Approaching

The EU AI Act's first hard transparency requirements land August 2: any AI system talking directly to users must disclose it's an AI, and synthetic audio, image, video, and text outputs must carry machine-readable watermarks. Systems already on the market get a grace period to December 2. For anyone deploying AI-generated content at scale, the watermarking requirement is the one to watch.
