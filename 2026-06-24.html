How the Music Industry Is Putting Itself Back Together
Ben Sisario · The New York Times · 2016

Sisario captured a genuine turning point: after a decade of piracy-driven collapse, streaming hadn't just stopped the bleeding — it was rebuilding the industry around entirely new economics. The piece documents the precise moment when access replaced ownership as the dominant model, with all the power shifts that entailed. Labels learned to love the thing they'd fought, artists learned to measure success in plays rather than sales.

Joe Dunthorne's essay in the NYRB opens with the New York School's defining paradox: a movement that refused to admit it was one.
