In a world of 'algorithmic culture,' music critics fight for relevance
Marc Hogan · Columbia Journalism Review · 2019

Hogan identified the emerging split between algorithmic curation and critical judgment back in 2019 — noting that while critics can welcome discourse and discomfort, recommendation engines are built to avoid offense. His argument that criticism's function was shifting from consumer guide to counter-algorithmic practice now reads less like prediction than diagnosis.
