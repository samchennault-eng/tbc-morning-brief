Friction-Free Racism
Meredith Whittaker · Real Life Magazine · 2020

Whittaker's 2020 essay argues that the seamlessness promised by platforms comes at a cost: eliminating the friction points where genuine human encounter occurs. The convenience of algorithmic mediation doesn't just smooth transactions — it removes the moments of difference, discomfort, and negotiation that constitute actual social life. When platforms optimize for frictionlessness, they're often optimizing away the human.
