The Midlife Crisis of the American Restaurant Review
Kat Kinsman · Los Angeles Review of Books · 2019

Kinsman diagnosed the moment restaurant criticism stopped being a verdict on a plate and became an argument about labor, access, and who gets to define good taste. The star rating gave way to the systemic read: kitchens as workplaces, dining rooms as sites of power. Her point is that the shift wasn't decline but maturation, criticism catching up to the culture it covers. The same reckoning has come for every field where a critic once just said whether they liked it.

The Haunting Music in the 'Odyssey' Siren Scene Has Ancient Roots

Joshua Barone, who covers classical music for the Times, breaks down the one moment from Nolan's "Odyssey" that fans can't stop looping on TikTok: the siren scene. Too Lost acquires Australia's GYRO Group – in first acquisition since nine-figure raise

Here's what that nine-figure raise was actually for: Too Lost is now deploying capital, and the first move is geographic. Music Business Worldwide reports the New York distributor has acquired Brisbane's GYRO.Group, planting a flag across Australia, New Zealand, and the broader Asia-Pacific. It's a signal that the well-funded indie distribution players intend to grow by buying regional footholds, not just signing artists.

CVC CAPITAL PARTNERS ACQUIRES MAJORITY STAKE IN DISTROKID

Private equity has decided independent distribution is worth owning: Music Business Worldwide reports CVC Capital Partners is taking a majority stake in DistroKid via its ninth fund, with longtime backer Insight Partners keeping a significant minority. The pricing engine that lets bedroom artists reach every DSP for a flat annual fee now answers to a firm that will want that flywheel spinning faster.
