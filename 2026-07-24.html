Communities, Audiences, and Scale
Clay Shirky · Shirky.com · 2002

Shirky drew the line two decades ago: a group small enough that members know each other is a community, and past a certain size it becomes an audience whose members relate to the center, not to each other. Growth isn't neutral scaling. It's a structural phase change that kills the thing people showed up for. Anyone promising both intimacy and reach is selling a contradiction, and Shirky already did the math.
