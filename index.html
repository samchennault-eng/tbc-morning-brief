In the Age of Streaming, Do Music Critics Still Matter?
CBC Arts · CBC Arts · 2023

CBC Arts staged a useful provocation: in a world where algorithms surface music and listeners curate their own taste, does professional criticism retain any function beyond nostalgia? The piece captures critics and artists circling the same anxious question without resolution. What lingers is less any answer than the underlying tension—whether cultural authority ever resided in critics themselves or simply in the scarcity of platforms they once controlled.

Ted Gioia uses Sports Illustrated's collapse as a case study in brand suicide by AI substitution. The argument: reputation built over decades can be destroyed in a single editorial decision.
