The Great Unbundling
Ben Thompson · Stratechery · 2017

Thompson's argument was structural: the internet doesn't just distribute content differently, it unbundles the economics that held legacy media together. Newspapers sold classifieds to fund foreign bureaus; albums forced listeners to buy filler for singles. Once each component could be delivered separately, specialized competitors could cherry-pick the profitable pieces and leave incumbents with the costs.

The Metaphysics of Sonny Rollins
