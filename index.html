The Sonic Identity Crisis
Hanif Abdurraqib · MTV News · 2018

Abdurraqib traced how streaming's algorithmic playlists dissolved the genre boundaries that once gave listeners tribal identity markers. When everything becomes mood-based background, the question isn't what you listen to but whether sonic taste still constitutes selfhood at all. The piece remains essential reading for anyone watching identity politics collide with infinite musical access.
