What Is a Record in the Age of Streaming?
Micah Singleton · The Verge · 2015

Singleton's 2015 piece asked a question the industry still hasn't answered: if streaming incentivizes singles and playlists, does the album remain a meaningful artistic unit? The piece arrived just as label strategies began shifting toward front-loaded tracklists and playlist-friendly sequencing — early signs of how distribution mechanics quietly reshape creative decisions.
