How TikTok Broke the Music Industry
Kyle Chayka · The New Yorker · 2024

Chayka traces how TikTok restructured music promotion around virality — artists now optimize for fifteen-second hooks rather than albums, labels chase trending sounds rather than developing talent, and careers spike and collapse in algorithmic cycles. The piece documents a fundamental shift: when discovery is governed by engagement metrics, the entire creative economy reorganizes around what spreads rather than what endures.

Tara Clerkin Trio — "Lazy Daisy"

via Editor's Music Recs

The Bristol trio's collage-like sound drifts between minimalist jazz, avant-pop, and trip-hop, built from hours of improvisation and layering around Clerkin's daydreamy vocals. Found sounds—scaffolding clangs, footsteps, chimes—enhance the uncanny atmosphere, while loop pedals allow three members to fill space like eight. Their forthcoming album Somewhere Good marks a more structured, pop-oriented turn while retaining the signature scatter of oddball textures.
