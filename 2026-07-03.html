Emo: Where the Girls Aren't
Jessica Hopper · Punk Planet / Rookie Mag · 2003

Hopper's argument landed because it refused the easy target. She wasn't cataloguing misogynist lyrics so much as diagnosing a structural erasure: emo positioned women only as the wound, the ex, the object men sang about, never as authors of their own experience or even as fans worth addressing. Two decades on, the piece remains the sharpest account of how a scene can claim emotional honesty while writing half its audience out of the story entirely.
