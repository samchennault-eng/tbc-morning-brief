How to Cultivate Taste in the Age of Algorithms
Kyle Chayka · Behavioral Scientist · 2024

Chayka's argument that algorithmic curation flattens aesthetic judgment by optimizing for engagement over genuine discovery remains the sharpest articulation of what we lose when recommendation engines replace cultural gatekeepers. His distinction between passive consumption and active taste-making—the deliberate work of developing preferences through friction and surprise—offers a framework for thinking about any system that promises to know what we want before we do.
