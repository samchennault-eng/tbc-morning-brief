The Celestial Jukebox
Charles C. Mann · The Atlantic · 2000

Mann saw it coming: the "celestial jukebox" that would let anyone access any song instantly would gut the economics of ownership while creating new gatekeepers. His 2000 Atlantic piece mapped the fault lines between access and control that would define digital culture — written when Napster was still a dorm-room insurgency and streaming was science fiction.
