Could Internet Culture Be Different?
Ethan Zuckerman · The New York Review of Books · 2022

Zuckerman's core move is to treat the internet we have as a choice, not a destiny. The ad-driven, engagement-maximizing platforms that dominate culture weren't the only possible outcome: they were built by specific decisions that could have gone another way, and could still be rebuilt. He points to public-interest alternatives, from community archives to municipally funded networks, that model what participation might look like when attention isn't the product.

08 All Music is Local Music (and a Lot of It’s Folk) 12 pieces

The hottest genre of 2026 is…folk music? Musicians from different scenes and disparate countries are exploring and reshaping regional, traditional music forms. Sometimes this is explicitly under the banner of folk music, while other times it reflects other idioms and traditions, but, regardless of the genre tag, it's music that creates a bridge to the future by looking at the past.

NPR Music

Playing 'La Bamba' for an hour? Must be a Mexican fandango!

In Veracruz, the clock tower on the central plaza plays "La Bamba" every hour, and it isn't the Ritchie Valens version. The original belongs to son jarocho, the region's music, played at fandangos where two dozen jarana players can stretch a three-chord song to 15 minutes.
