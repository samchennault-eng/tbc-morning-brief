Music Criticism in the Time of Stans and Haters
Rawiya Kameir · Columbia Journalism Review · 2019

Kameir named the shift early: fandom stopped being an audience and became an enforcement arm. A negative review no longer just annoys the artist; it triggers the mob. She traced how critics learned to write defensively, softening judgment to avoid the pile-on, and how that self-censorship quietly reshapes what gets said about music at all. The critic's job was always to risk being wrong in public. Now the risk comes with a target on your back.
