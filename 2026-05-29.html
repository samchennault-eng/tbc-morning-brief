Why Is Music Journalism Collapsing?
Ted Gioia · The Honest Broker · 2024

Gioia traces how streaming atomized the music industry's attention economy — when albums became disaggregated playlists, the publications that contextualized them lost their reason to exist. His argument isn't nostalgic; it's structural. The same forces that made music free made writing about music worthless. What happens to any creative field when the infrastructure for discussing it disappears?
